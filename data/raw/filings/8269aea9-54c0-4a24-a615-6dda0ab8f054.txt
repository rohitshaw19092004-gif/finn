L&T bags ( Significant*) order from 
Noida Metro for Aqua Line Extension 
Mumbai, November 9, 2026: The Heavy Civil Infrastructure business vertical of L&T has 
secured an order from Noida Metro Rail Corporation Ltd for part design and construction of 
the Noida Metro Aqua Line Extension Project.  
The project scope encompasses part design and construction of a 15.1 km elevated viaduct 
from Botanical Garden to Noida Sector -142, and from Depot Station to Boraki. The scope 
includes the construction of 10 elevated metro stations, including architectural finishes. 
Noida Metro Aqua Line Extension is poised to become a landmark urban transit project, 
significantly enhancing regional connectivity and commuter accessibility. The project will 
play a crucial role in supporting Noida's expanding urban landscape and is to be completed 
within a stringent timeline of 36 months.
