Sub: Change in Directors - Outcome of the Board Meeting held today i.e. 29th October 2026 
 
Dear Sir / Madam, 
 
1. Retirement of Mr. Noel N. Tata as the Chairman  
 
The Company has adopted the Tata Group Governance Guidelines, which, inter alia, prescribe 
the requirements related to  Board Composition, Board Committees and the  retirement of 
directors. 
 
In accordance with the aforesaid Guidelines, Mr. Noel N. Tata (DIN: 00024713) will retire from 
the office of Chairman and Director of the Company with effect from the close of business 
hours on 11th November 2026. 
 
Mr. Tata has been associated with the Company since 1997 and has provided exemplary 
leadership and strategic direction throughout his tenure. Under his stewardship, the Company 
has achieved significant milestones, strengthened its market position, and delivered sustained 
growth and value for its stakeholders. The Board of Directors places on record its deepest 
appreciation and profound gratitude for his invaluable contributions to the Company  over the 
past 29 years.  
 
In recognition of his substantial and valuable contribution to the Company , the Board has 
appointed Mr. Noel N. Tata as Chairman Emeritus with effect from 12th November 2026.   
  
2. Appointment of Mr. Bahram Vakil as the Chairman  
 
Consequent to the above, the Board has  appointed Mr. Bahram Vakil (DIN: 00283980) as 
Chairman of the Company with effect from 12th November 2026. 
 
3. Appointment of Mr . Rajiv Dube  as an Additional Director (Non - Executive, Non-
Independent) 
 
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board 
has approved the appointment of Mr . Rajiv Dube (DIN: 00021796) as an Additional Director 
(Non-Executive, Non-Independent) of the Company with effect from 30th October 2026, subject 
to the approval of the shareholders.  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
4. Appointment of Ms. Kalpana Merchant as an Independent Director 
 
Based on the recommendation of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee, the Board 
has approved the appointment of Ms. Kalpana Merchant (DIN: 00827907) as an Additional 
Director of the Company with effect from 30th October 2026.  Further, the Board has also 
approved her appointment as an Independent Director of the Company for a term of three 
years commencing from 30th October 2026  up to 2 9th October 20 29 (both days inclusive) , 
subject to the approval of the shareholders.  
 
The details required under Regulation 30 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing 
Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, read with Schedule III thereto and 
the SEBI Master Circular dated 30th January 2026, are enclosed herewith. 
 
The Board Meeting commenced at 12.15 p.m. and concluded at 3.45 p.m. 
 
The aforesaid information is also available on the Company's website www.trentlimited.com. 
  
 This is for your information and records.

Profiles 
Mr. Rajiv Dube 
Mr. Rajiv Dube is an Indian industry veteran with over 40 years of multi -sector experience, having 
served at the highest echelons of two Indian conglomerates - Tata and Aditya Birla. 
An engineer and a post-graduate in Business Management, he began his career on the shop floor of 
Tata Motors and rose to be President of its car business before joining the group board of the Aditya 
Birla group as an executive director, on which he remaine d for nearly nine years. He has served on 
several other Indian and foreign boards in various capacities over 25 years and brings deep insights 
in corporate governance, management and transformation of businesses as varied as auto, metals, 
mining, power and renewables, cement, trading, textiles, retail and financial services. 
An avid sustainability champion, he was an alternate Council Member of the World Business Council 
for Sustainable Development (WBCSD) Geneva and has been active on several industry 
bodies.  Currently, Mr. Dube is an Independent Director on Boards, an advis ory board member and 
professor of practice at the Deakin Business School, Melbourne and a senior advisor to firms in India 
and Australia. 
Ms. Kalpana Merchant  
Ms. Kalpana Merchant has over 45 years of experience in the legal profession.  She has significant 
experience in dealing with the commercial property matters. Her experience also spans diverse 
practice areas, including infrastructure, project finance and banking, dispute resolution, capital 
markets, and mergers and acquisitions, including joint ventures.  
She has been associated with, and has been a partner at, Kanga & Co., AZB & Partners and Jerome 
Merchant + Partners. She holds a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree and a Bachelor of Arts degree with 
a major in Psychology and is a qualified solicitor.
