Annexure 
 
Shri P B Rangarao, holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engi neering from 
Regional Engineering College, Warangal, and a Post Graduate Dip loma in Business 
Management from XIME, Bengaluru. He has also completed a Leader ship 
Development Program from the Indian Institute of Management (IIM), Ahmedabad. 
Shri P B Rangarao joined HAL on July 17, 1995, as part of the 3 rd batch of Design 
Trainees and brings with him more than three decades of extensive experience across 
multiple domains within the organization. Over the course of hi s 31 years career at 
HAL, he has served in several ke y roles across departments incl uding Plant 
Maintenance, ALH Manufacturing Shops, Ch/Ck Transmission and Blade Shop, Head 
of Ch/CK project, Outsourcing, and Planning. He later took over as General Manager, 
Helicopter Division, and was subsequently elevated to the post of Executive Director, 
Helicopter Division. Prior to taking charge as Director (Operations), he served as Chief 
Executive Officer (Helicopter Complex) of the Company. 
Some of his achievements during his tenure in Hindustan Aeronau tics Limited are, 
operationalization of the new Helicopter Division at Tumkuru leading to the production 
rollout of the first batch of Light Utility Helicopters (LUH), successfully commissioning 
of LCH-LSP Bases for the Indian Army and Indian Air Force, conclusion of the 25 ALH 
Mk III Contract for IA in Mar 2024 and 156 LCH SP contract for IA and IAF in Mar 
2025, re-establishment of supply chains for Cheetah and Chetak helicopters following 
the closure of Airbus facilities at France, enhancement of production capacity through 
effective public-private partnerships, addressing obsolescence challenges through 
focused indigenization initiatives, streamlining of manufacturi ng processes for 
improved production efficiency of ALH components. 
Shri P B Rangarao brings deep expertise in end-to-end process o ptimization, 
manufacturing systems, quality control, and supply chain manage ment. His strategic 
vision and execution capabilities are key assets in driving HAL ’s transition to higher 
levels of production efficiency and technological self-reliance.
